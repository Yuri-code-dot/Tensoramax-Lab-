 04 / TXB

TXB

A failed local-first LLM character and agent experiment. The prototype pushed a small model, constrained hardware and an oversized ambition into the same pipeline. It broke under its own constraints: limited model capacity, weak long-context retention, brittle instruction following, and too much orchestration for too little compute. It failed as a dependable system, but the failure exposed the engineering limits worth studying.
